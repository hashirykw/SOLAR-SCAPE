Our story

Energy from natural resources.

SolarScape Solutions® was founded with a vision to change the way we power our world. We are a complete solar power installation company specialising in industrial installations and after-sales service.

We bring newer technology to Pakistan, including micro inverters alongside conventional inverter systems, and as a FOX ESS Gold Distributor and net metering specialists we handle the equipment, the installation and the paperwork.

From our primary office in Karachi we make it easy for residents, public entities and businesses to minimise the hassle and maximise the benefits of solar, with teams in Dubai and the United Kingdom.

Milestones

A young brand, an experienced team.

SolarScape launched in 2024 as a project of the Muqeet Group of Companies. Here is what has happened since.

  1. 2024

    SolarScape Solutions® launches

    A project of the Muqeet Group of Companies, with its primary office in Karachi and a team bringing more than 17 years of working experience.

  2. Sept 2024

    FOX ESS distributorship signed

    A formal agreement at the Karachi Marriott Hotel makes SolarScape a FOX ESS Gold Distributor, bringing global inverter technology to homes and businesses in Pakistan.

  3. Sept 2024

    House of Italia, 50 kW

    A 50 kW solar installation in partnership with House of Italia, completed on one of the fastest schedules the team has run.

  4. Today

    Three countries, growing client list

    Offices in Karachi, Business Bay Dubai and Romford UK, serving clients including Dawlance, Pharmevo, British Pathway and Jamtex International.
