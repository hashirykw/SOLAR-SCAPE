Net metering

How the meter runs backwards.

Your panels feed the building first. Whatever you don't use flows to the grid through a two-way meter, and is credited against what you draw at night.

  1. SunlightPanels turn light into DC power.
  2. InverterConverts it to AC your building can use.
  3. Your buildingUses solar first, every daylight hour.
  4. Net meterCounts units both ways and credits the export.
