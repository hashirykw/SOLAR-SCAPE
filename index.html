Technology

The equipment decides the output.

We signed our FOX ESS distributorship at the Karachi Marriott Hotel, and we install both conventional string inverters and micro inverters, matched to tier-one modules from Jinko Solar and LONGi.

Conventional

One inverter per string. The most economical choice for big, open, unshaded roofs.

Micro inverter

Conversion at every panel. Shade or dust on one module never drags down the rest.
