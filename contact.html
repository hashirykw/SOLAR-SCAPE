Quick answers.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Share your bill or units and the team will set up a consultation and, where needed, a site survey.

When can I reach support?

Customer support runs 24/7 on +92 309 2001444 and support@solarscape.pk.

Yes. We serve businesses across Pakistan from the Karachi head office, with teams in Dubai and the UK.
